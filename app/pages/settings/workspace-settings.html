      Roles

      A role is a ready-made set of access. Built-in roles are the same on every brand and can’t be changed — make your own to give someone exactly what they need.

        Marketing commission

        What you pay your marketing agency. It is taken off revenue everywhere profit is shown — the Overview, profit ROAS and the break-even the assistant works from.

        %

        Share of net revenue, shipping not included.

        EGP

        A whole month costs exactly this. 7 days of a 31-day month cost 7/31 of it.

        Saving changes every profit figure in this workspace, for every period — past ones too.

        Logos

        They replace the generated initials across the workspace. A transparent PNG or SVG works best — the sidebar sits on a dark background.
